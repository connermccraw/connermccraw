## Hi, I'm Conner 👋

Founder & CEO of [9+1AI](https://www.9plus1.ai), based in Winston-Salem, NC.

We're building **eBNP**: AI that reads the heart-failure signal hidden in an ordinary ECG. Elevated BNP is the lab test clinicians use to catch heart failure, but it needs a blood draw. Our model estimates it from the ECG waveform alone, so the screen can happen anywhere an ECG already exists, from a hospital 12-lead to a wearable.

**What I spend my days on**

- Clinical validation with academic health systems and the FDA's TAP program
- Getting a regulated AI device from research code to a quality-managed product
- Partnerships with the companies that already collect ECGs at scale
